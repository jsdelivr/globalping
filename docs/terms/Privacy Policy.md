By using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Our Service may contain links to other sites that are not operated by us. If you click on a third party link, you will be directed to that third party's site. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies or practices of any third party sites or services.

## 2. Information Collection

1. We collect the following types of information depending on which Services you use or access:

   1. **Service Usage Data:** We may collect information about how the Services are accessed and used. This may include your IP address, browser or client type and version, referrer domain, URLs visited on globalping.io, timestamps, unique device or request identifiers, and other diagnostic, security, and network data.

   2. **Account and Contact Data:** When you create an account, we collect information such as your username, email address, and authentication credentials. We may also collect your email address and other information you provide when you contact us.

   3. **Measurement Data:** We collect the parameters submitted for a measurement and the results generated by it. These may include targets, requested locations, measurement options, custom HTTP headers, probe information, network paths, DNS responses, and other measurement output. Measurement Data is public by design, may be accessed by anyone with the measurement ID, and may be published or made discoverable. Do not include credentials or confidential information in Measurement Data, and avoid including unnecessary personal data.

   4. **MCP Usage Data:** When you use our hosted MCP service, we may record tool names, information submitted to and returned by MCP tools (including Measurement Data), timestamps, duration, errors, MCP client name and version, User-Agent, request and network metadata, an internal session identifier, a generic authentication category, and the OAuth client ID where applicable. Request metadata may include IP, country, request, or session-related identifiers supplied by your client or our infrastructure. If you use the `get_more_tools` tool, we may also collect the missing-capability description submitted to that tool.

2. We exclude the authentication credentials provided in the transport-level MCP `Authorization` header from MCP Usage Data. We do not generally detect or remove secrets included in ordinary tool inputs, measurement targets, query values, custom headers, or results. You must not submit credentials or other confidential values in those fields.

## 3. Use of Data

1. In general terms, we may use the collected data to:

   1. provide and maintain our Service,
   2. allow you to participate in interactive features of our Services when you choose to do so,
   3. gather analysis or valuable information so that we can improve our Services,
   4. monitor the usage of our Services,
   5. detect, prevent, and address technical issues.

2. We use MCP Usage Data to understand which MCP tools and clients are used, diagnose failed or slow calls, improve tool descriptions, behavior, and reliability, understand requested capabilities, and protect and operate the Services. We do not use MCP Usage Data for advertising or sell it.

3. Please note that our Services are not intended for use by children, and we do not knowingly collect or use personal information relating to children. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your children have provided us with personal data, please contact us. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from children without verification of parental consent, we take steps to remove that information from our servers.

## 4. Retention of Data

1. We will retain your personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy.

2. We will retain and use your personal data to the extent necessary to comply with our legal obligations (for example, if we are required to retain your data to comply with applicable laws), resolve disputes, and enforce our legal agreements and policies.

3. Measurement Data is public. We retain it to provide results and to operate and protect the Services. The current standard period for which measurements are available is stated in our [API documentation](https://globalping.io/docs/api.globalping.io). We may retain data longer where necessary to investigate abuse, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations. Anyone who accesses public Measurement Data may store a copy independently, potentially indefinitely; we cannot control or delete those copies. We may retain measurement-derived operational or aggregated statistics for longer; these do not contain MCP client or session details.

4. We retain MCP Usage Data for up to 60 days, unless a longer period is needed for a specific security investigation or legal obligation.

5. We retain other non-aggregated Usage Data only for as long as needed for operational analysis, abuse prevention, security, and service improvement, unless we are legally required to keep it longer.
